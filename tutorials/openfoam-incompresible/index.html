OpenFOAM · Incompressible flow

A tutorial series on incompressible flow with OpenFOAM, starting from the case every textbook solves: the straight pipe. Each tutorial brings the complete case, the mesh from Gmsh, and a validation against the exact solution or against measured correlations.

On Windows, with blueCFD-Core. Every figure and number comes from an actual run of OpenFOAM 12 in blueCFD-Core 2024, with Gmsh 4.15.2 and ParaView 5.11.2.
