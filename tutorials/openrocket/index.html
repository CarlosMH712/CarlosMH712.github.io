OpenRocket

A tutorial series on OpenRocket for the course project: a low-power rocket for an Estes B or C motor, built from a cardboard tube, 3D-printed parts and balsa or MDF fins, with a parachute and a retained motor.

The model is the rocket that will be built. Every part goes in with its real material and mass, because the stability margin depends on all of them. Every figure and number comes from OpenRocket 24.12 itself.
