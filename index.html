About

Academic profile

I teach aerospace engineering and develop educational resources that connect theory, computation, simulation, and engineering practice. My work focuses on making advanced technical concepts accessible through structured notes, interactive calculators, and reproducible computational tools.

Main areas

  • Aerodynamics and compressible flow
  • Aircraft propulsion
  • Flight dynamics and control
  • Numerical methods and scientific computing
  • Mathematical modeling and nonlinear PDEs

Research

Mathematical modeling and scientific computing

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations, fractional models, Davey–Stewartson-type systems, spectral methods, conservation properties, and numerical simulation.

Research topics

  • Nonlinear partial differential equations
  • Riesz fractional derivatives
  • FFT-based spectral methods
  • Operator splitting and conservative schemes
  • Scientific computing in MATLAB and Python
