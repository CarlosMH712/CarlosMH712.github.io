Physical picture

Supersonic compression around a concave corner

A supersonic stream cannot adjust smoothly upstream of a finite compression corner. The disturbances accumulate into an oblique shock that turns and compresses the flow.

\[ \boxed{\mu=\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{M_1}\right)} \]

The Mach angle \(\mu\) is the limiting wave angle for an infinitesimal disturbance. A finite compressive shock satisfies \(\beta>\mu\).

Velocity decomposition

Normal and tangential components

\[ V_{n1}=V_1\sin\beta, \qquad V_{t1}=V_1\cos\beta \]
\[ V_{n2}=V_2\sin(\beta-\theta), \qquad V_{t2}=V_2\cos(\beta-\theta) \]
\[ \boxed{ M_{n1}=M_1\sin\beta, \qquad M_{n2}=M_2\sin(\beta-\theta) } \]
Central simplification. The tangential velocity remains constant, \(V_{t1}=V_{t2}\). Mass, normal momentum, and energy therefore reduce exactly to the normal-shock equations applied to \(M_{n1}\).

Jump relations

Normal-shock equations evaluated with \(M_{n1}\)

\[ \boxed{ \frac{p_2}{p_1} = 1+\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma+1}(M_{n1}^2-1) } \]
\[ \boxed{ \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1} = \frac{(\gamma+1)M_{n1}^2} {2+(\gamma-1)M_{n1}^2} } \]
\[ \boxed{ \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \frac{p_2/p_1}{\rho_2/\rho_1} } \]
\[ \boxed{ M_{n2}^2= \frac{1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_{n1}^2} {\gamma M_{n1}^2-\frac{\gamma-1}{2}} } \]
\[ \boxed{ M_2=\frac{M_{n2}}{\sin(\beta-\theta)} } \]

The total-pressure ratio is also obtained from the normal-shock relation evaluated at \(M_{n1}\). The total temperature remains constant, but \(p_0\) decreases.

Central relation

The \(\theta\)–\(\beta\)–\(M\) equation

\[ \boxed{ \tan\theta = 2\cot\beta\, \frac{M_1^2\sin^2\beta-1} {M_1^2[\gamma+\cos(2\beta)]+2} } \]

Given \(M_1\) and the required deflection \(\theta\), this nonlinear relation provides the admissible shock angles \(\beta\). Once \(\beta\) is known, the normal-component method determines the complete downstream state.

Compact geometric derivation
\[ \tan\beta=\frac{V_{n1}}{V_{t1}}, \qquad \tan(\beta-\theta)=\frac{V_{n2}}{V_{t2}}. \]

Since \(V_{t1}=V_{t2}\) and continuity gives \(V_{n2}/V_{n1}=\rho_1/\rho_2\),

\[ \tan(\beta-\theta)=\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2}\tan\beta. \]

Substitution of the normal-shock density ratio yields the classical equation.

Multiple solutions

Weak, strong, and detached shocks

Weak branch

Smaller \(\beta\), smaller \(M_{n1}\), moderate pressure rise, usually \(M_2>1\).

Strong branch

Larger \(\beta\), stronger compression, larger total-pressure loss, normally \(M_2<1\).

Engineering workflow

Calculation algorithm

1Specify \(M_1,\theta,\gamma\)
2Compute \(\mu=\sin^{-1}(1/M_1)\)
3Find \(\theta_{\max}\)
4Solve θ–β–M for weak/strong \(\beta\)
5Set \(M_{n1}=M_1\sin\beta\)
6Apply normal-shock relations
7Recover \(M_2\)
8Check branch and pressure loss
When \(\theta>\theta_{\max}\), a numerical root should not be forced. The physical result is a detached shock, outside the straight attached-shock model.

Worked example

Weak shock over a \(15^\circ\) wedge

Air at \(M_1=2.5\), \(p_1=80\ \text{kPa}\), and \(T_1=250\ \text{K}\) is turned through \(\theta=15^\circ\).

1
\[\beta_{\mathrm{weak}}\approx36.95^\circ.\]
2
\[M_{n1}=2.5\sin36.95^\circ\approx1.502.\]
3
\[\frac{p_2}{p_1}=2.4675,\quad \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=1.866,\quad \frac{T_2}{T_1}=1.32196.\]
4
\[M_{n2}\approx0.701,\qquad M_2=\frac{0.701}{\sin(36.95^\circ-15^\circ)}\approx1.873.\]
5
\[p_2\approx197.4\ \text{kPa},\qquad T_2\approx330.5\ \text{K},\qquad \frac{p_{0,2}}{p_{0,1}}\approx0.929.\]
\[ \boxed{ \beta\approx36.95^\circ,\quad M_2\approx1.87,\quad p_2\approx197.4\ \text{kPa},\quad T_2\approx330.5\ \text{K} } \]

Interpretación física

Compresión supersónica alrededor de una esquina cóncava

Una corriente supersónica no puede ajustarse suavemente aguas arriba de una compresión finita. Las perturbaciones se acumulan en una onda oblicua que desvía y comprime el flujo.

\[\boxed{\mu=\sin^{-1}(1/M_1)}\]

Una onda compresiva finita cumple \(\beta>\mu\).

Descomposición de velocidad

Componentes normal y tangencial

\[V_{n1}=V_1\sin\beta,\qquad V_{t1}=V_1\cos\beta\]
\[V_{n2}=V_2\sin(\beta-\theta),\qquad V_{t2}=V_2\cos(\beta-\theta)\]
\[\boxed{M_{n1}=M_1\sin\beta,\qquad M_{n2}=M_2\sin(\beta-\theta)}\]
Simplificación central. \(V_{t1}=V_{t2}\). Las ecuaciones restantes son las de un choque normal aplicadas a \(M_{n1}\).

Relaciones de salto

Choque normal evaluado con el Mach normal

\[\boxed{\frac{p_2}{p_1}=1+\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma+1}(M_{n1}^2-1)}\]
\[\boxed{\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=\frac{(\gamma+1)M_{n1}^2}{2+(\gamma-1)M_{n1}^2}}\]
\[\boxed{\frac{T_2}{T_1}=\frac{p_2/p_1}{\rho_2/\rho_1}}\]
\[\boxed{M_{n2}^2=\frac{1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_{n1}^2}{\gamma M_{n1}^2-\frac{\gamma-1}{2}}}\]
\[\boxed{M_2=\frac{M_{n2}}{\sin(\beta-\theta)}}\]

Relación central

Ecuación \(\theta\)–\(\beta\)–\(M\)

\[ \boxed{ \tan\theta = 2\cot\beta\, \frac{M_1^2\sin^2\beta-1} {M_1^2[\gamma+\cos(2\beta)]+2} } \]

Con \(M_1\) y \(\theta\) conocidos, se obtiene \(\beta\); después se calcula \(M_{n1}\) y se aplican las relaciones de choque normal.

Soluciones múltiples

Ramas débil, fuerte y choque desprendido

Rama débil

Menor \(\beta\), compresión moderada y normalmente \(M_2>1\).

Rama fuerte

Mayor \(\beta\), mayor pérdida de presión total y normalmente \(M_2<1\).

Si \(\theta>\theta_{\max}\), no existe una solución recta adherida: la onda se desprende.

Procedimiento

Algoritmo de cálculo

  1. Especificar \(M_1,\theta,\gamma\).
  2. Calcular \(\mu\) y \(\theta_{\max}\).
  3. Resolver la ecuación \(\theta\)–\(\beta\)–\(M\).
  4. Elegir la rama débil o fuerte.
  5. Calcular \(M_{n1}=M_1\sin\beta\).
  6. Aplicar choque normal.
  7. Recuperar \(M_2\) y evaluar la pérdida de \(p_0\).

Ejemplo resuelto

Choque débil sobre una cuña de \(15^\circ\)

\(M_1=2.5\), \(p_1=80\ \text{kPa}\), \(T_1=250\ \text{K}\), \(\theta=15^\circ\).

1
\[\beta\approx36.95^\circ,\qquad M_{n1}\approx1.502.\]
2
\[\frac{p_2}{p_1}=2.4675,\quad \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=1.866,\quad \frac{T_2}{T_1}=1.32196.\]
3
\[M_2\approx1.873,\quad p_2\approx197.4\ \text{kPa},\quad T_2\approx330.5\ \text{K}.\]
\[\boxed{\beta\approx36.95^\circ,\quad M_2\approx1.87,\quad p_{0,2}/p_{0,1}\approx0.929}\]
Caso de validación\(M_1=2.5\), \(\theta=15^\circ\), rama débil.

Kerninhalt

Schrägstoßwellen

Ein Schrägstoß wird mit der normalen Machzahl \(M_{n1}=M_1\sin\beta\) auf einen Normalstoß zurückgeführt.

\[\boxed{M_{n1}=M_1\sin\beta}\]
\[\boxed{\tan\theta=2\cot\beta\frac{M_1^2\sin^2\beta-1}{M_1^2[\gamma+\cos(2\beta)]+2}}\]
\[\boxed{M_2=\frac{M_{n2}}{\sin(\beta-\theta)}}\]
Für \(0<\theta<\theta_{\max}\) existieren eine schwache und eine starke Lösung. Oberhalb von \(\theta_{\max}\) löst sich die Stoßwelle vom Körper ab.
